02The gap

College teaches how AI works. Recruiters hire students who have built with it.

Syllabus

Course content trails the industry

Agents, RAG and LangGraph went from research papers to job descriptions in about three years. Most college courses have not caught up yet.

Hiring

Recruiters ask to see projects

A GitHub repo with a working RAG app and a live demo link says more than a list of completed courses.

Interviews

The vocabulary is a filter

Interviewers check whether you can talk about tokens, embeddings, evals, latency and hallucinations with confidence.

5X Minds closes that gap with practical sessions on the tools companies use today, and a portfolio of projects by the final day.

04The roadmap

Six weeks, one skill stacked on the last.

Week 1

Setup & Python for AI

Install the toolchain, refresh Python and start coding with an AI assistant.

Week 2

LLMs & frontier models

How LLMs work, prompting, and the GPT, Claude and Gemini APIs.

Week 3

Open source & Hugging Face

Run open models locally, Transformers pipelines and model selection.

Week 4

RAG with LangChain

Embeddings, vector stores and a chatbot over your own documents.

Week 5

Agents with LangGraph

Tools, memory, multi-agent workflows and how to evaluate them.

Week 6

Fine-tuning, images & demo day

LoRA on your dataset, image models, deployment and your capstone pitch.

The 4-week Sprint follows the core path through Python, LLMs, RAG and agents. The 6-week Deep Track adds open-source models, fine-tuning, image models and demo day.
